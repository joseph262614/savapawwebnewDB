================================================================================
APPENDIX: SAMPLE INPUT AND OUTPUT
================================================================================
Project Title: SAVEAPAW: A Community-Driven Animal Welfare, Adoption, and Rescue 
               Platform with AI-Assisted Support (Mobile & Web Admin Application)
Proponents:    Capstone Research & Development Team
Document Type: Capstone Manuscript Appendix / Operational User Guide
================================================================================

This appendix presents sample inputs and outputs of the SAVEAPAW Community-Driven 
Animal Welfare, Adoption, and Rescue Platform. Each sample illustrates the exact 
data and operational actions performed by users (Input) alongside the corresponding 
system behavior, visual interface, and database state returned by the platform (Output). 
The workflows demonstrate role-based access control, distinguishing between Guest Users 
(unauthenticated visitors) and Verified Members/Rescuers across core features.

================================================================================
1. GUEST USER MODULE (UNAUTHENTICATED ACCESS)
================================================================================

Sample 1.1: Guest Navigation (Home, Community, and Donation Access)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Guest (Unauthenticated Visitor)
Purpose:   Allowing public citizens to explore shelter activities, read educational 
           community posts, and donate without account friction.

Input:
The guest launches the mobile application and taps "Continue as Guest" (or skips 
the login prompt). The guest browses the bottom navigation bar and selects "Home", 
"Community", or "Donate".

Output:
The system loads public platform features:
• Home Screen: Displays the dynamic hero banner ("Find Your Best Friend"), 
  Animal Rescue Team Metroeast (ART) highlight reels, recent outreach mission photos, 
  and partner NGO spotlights.
• Community Screen: Displays official shelter announcements, pet care articles, 
  and public community stories.
• Donation Screen: Displays verified GCash QR codes, bank transfer details, and 
  in-kind donation guidelines for food, medicine, and bedding.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 1.2: Role-Based Interface Visibility & Guest Gating
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Guest (Unauthenticated Visitor)
Purpose:   Enforcing platform security and clean UX by completely hiding protected 
           member modules (Adoption Center, Report Stray, Reported Pets in Need, 
           and Messages) from unauthenticated visitors.

Input:
The guest user navigates through the bottom navigation bar and opens the application 
navigation drawer, then taps the "Log In / Sign Up" option in the drawer.

Output:
The system dynamically restricts the user interface based on authentication state:
• Hidden Modules: The "Adoption Center", "Report Stray", "Reported Pets in Need", 
  and "Messages" options are completely hidden from the guest's bottom navigation bar, 
  home screen, and navigation drawer.
• Visible Modules: Only public navigation items ("Home", "Community", "Donate") and 
  a highlighted "Log In / Sign Up" drawer tile are accessible.
• System Reaction: Tapping "Log In / Sign Up" opens the Login & Registration portal, 
  allowing the guest to authenticate and unlock full access to the Adoption Center, 
  pet rescue reporting, and community foster features.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. MEMBER REGISTRATION & IDENTITY VERIFICATION
================================================================================

Sample 2.1: User Account Registration & Role Selection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: New Member
Purpose:   Creating a secure, role-differentiated user profile.

Input:
The new user navigates to the Registration screen and enters:
• Full Name: "Maria Santos"
• Username: "mariasantos"
• Email Address: "maria.santos@gmail.com"
• Contact Number: "09171234567"
• Password: "••••••••••••" (minimum 6 characters)
• Role Selection: Selects "Adopter" (or "Foster", "Volunteer", "Partner")
• Legal Age & Policy Confirmation: Checks the checkbox agreeing to the Terms and 
  Conditions (confirming minimum age of 18 years old) and taps "Create Account".

Output:
The system:
1. Validates input formatting and verifies username/email uniqueness.
2. Creates a user record in Firebase Authentication.
3. Initializes a database profile at `/users/{uid}` with `isVerified: false`, 
   `isAdmin: false`, `isBanned: false`, and assigned role.
4. Automatically signs the user in and redirects to the identity verification banner.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 2.2: Identity Accreditation via Document Scanner
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Registered Member (Pending Verification)
Purpose:   Preventing fraudulent adoptions and spam reporting through government ID 
           and facial biometric verification.

Input:
The user opens the Profile screen, taps "Get Verified", selects "Government-Issued ID", 
uses the integrated Google ML Kit Document Scanner to capture their ID (with automatic 
edge detection and perspective rectification), captures a live facial selfie, and taps 
"Submit Verification".

Output:
The system:
1. Encrypts and uploads the ID document and selfie to Firebase Cloud Storage at 
   `/verification/{uid}/`.
2. Creates an audit record under `/verificationRequests/{uid}` with timestamp and 
   status "pending".
3. Updates the user's profile badge to "Verification Pending", alerting the user that 
   an administrator will review the submission within 24 to 48 hours.

3. VERIFIED MEMBER & RESCUER MODULES
================================================================================

Sample 3.1: Adoption Center Catalog Browsing & Inquiry Submission
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Verified Member / Adopter
Purpose:   Facilitating ethical, fee-free pet adoptions between adopters and fosters.

Input:
The verified adopter opens the "Adoption Center" tab, filters by category ("Dogs"), 
swipes through pet cards, inspects medical badges (Vaccinated, Neutered, Microchipped), 
taps on pet "Bella", reviews the personality description, clicks "Adopt", enters an 
introductory message: "Hi, I have a fenced yard in Marikina and would love to adopt Bella!", 
and clicks "Submit Inquiry".

Output:
The system:
1. Validates that the pet is currently available (`isAdopted: false`).
2. Creates an adoption inquiry record in `/adoption_inquiries/{inquiryId}` linked to 
   both the adopter and foster parent.
3. Automatically opens a dedicated real-time chat channel between the adopter and foster.
4. Displays a confirmation snackbar: "Inquiry sent! You can now chat directly with Bella's foster."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 3.2: Custody Agreement, Pickup Scheduling (5-Day Window), and Adoption Pass
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Verified Adopter & Foster Parent
Purpose:   Enforcing formal custody commitments and providing a verifiable handover 
           voucher with a mandatory 5-day retrieval scheduling window.

Input:
Following mutual chat screening, the foster approves the inquiry. The adopter taps 
"Proceed to Adoption Agreement", reviews the legal terms (prohibiting resale, neglect, 
and requiring humane treatment), checks "I Agree to Adoption Responsibilities", 
selects a retrieval date within the strict 5-day window starting from the agreement 
date (e.g., choosing within November 8 – November 12), and clicks "Agree & Confirm Retrieval Date".

Output:
The system:
1. Enforces the strict 5-day scheduling limit: all dates outside this 5-day window 
   are disabled in the calendar, preventing selection of subsequent months or years 
   to ensure unclaimed pets can be offered to other approved applicants.
2. Generates a unique 5-character alphanumeric Adoption Pass code (e.g., "K9B7X").
3. Generates a scannable QR/barcode containing the inquiry ID, adopter UID, and verification hash.
4. Displays the on-screen Adoption Pass modal with instructions:
   "Adoption and screening venue: Marikina. Present this 5-character code (K9B7X) during 
   on-site screening for verification. Remember, Save-A-Paws adoptions are 100% free!"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 3.3: Reporting a Stray or Distressed Animal with GPS Geotagging
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Verified Member / Volunteer / Partner
Purpose:   Rapid emergency dispatch of rescue teams to injured or abandoned animals.

Input:
A member spots an injured puppy, taps "Report Stray", captures a live photograph using 
the in-app camera, allows high-precision GPS positioning, selects animal type ("Dog"), 
urgency level ("Emergency / Injured"), types description: "Injured left paw, near Marikina 
Sports Center", and taps "Submit Report".

Output:
The system:
1. Uploads the incident photo to Cloud Storage.
2. Records the report under `/reports/{reportId}` with GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude), 
   reverse-geocoded address, user ID, and status "pending".
3. Plots an urgent incident pin on the interactive OpenStreetMap live incident map.
4. Broadcasts a heads-up rescue alert notification to all verified volunteers and rescue 
   partners located within a 10-kilometer radius.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 3.4: Rescue Dispatch, On-Site Rescue Confirmation & Resolution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Volunteer / Rescue Partner
Purpose:   Closing the rescue loop with verifiable photographic proof.

Input:
A field volunteer opens the "Reported Pets in Need" hub, selects the incident marker at 
Marikina Sports Center, taps "Respond to Incident", arrives at the scene, taps 
"Confirm Rescued", takes a live camera photo of the safely secured animal, and clicks 
"Submit Rescue Proof".

Output:
The system:
1. Updates the report status to "Rescued" and locks the record against further edits.
2. Attaches the timestamped verification photo to the report history.
3. Changes the map pin icon from an alert red badge to a resolved green badge.
4. Publishes a rescue success event to the community Activity Feed, crediting the volunteer.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 3.5: Conversational AI Assistance (PawPal AI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Any Authenticated User (Adopter, Foster, Volunteer)
Purpose:   Instant 24/7 guidance regarding adoption screening locations and pet health triage.

Input:
The user taps the floating PawPal AI puppy widget and selects the suggested question:
"Where does adoption and screening happen?"

Output:
PawPal AI generates an immediate, warm response:
"Adoption and screening take place in Marikina! We do not have a permanent designated 
facility yet, but our team will announce the exact venue in the app as soon as one of 
our dedicated volunteers, rescue teams, or local government partners provides the location 
for the screening and handover. Adoptions on Save-A-Paws are completely 100% free with zero fees!"

4. ADMINISTRATOR WORKSTATION MODULE (ADMIN PC & MANAGEMENT PORTAL)
================================================================================

Sample 4.1: Member Identity Verification Audit & Accreditation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Shelter Administrator / System Operator
Purpose:   Preventing fraudulent pet adoptions and unverified rescue claims by auditing 
           submitted government IDs and facial biometric photos.

Input:
The administrator logs into the Save-A-Paws Admin PC workstation, clicks "Verification 
Requests" on the navigation panel, views the pending request queue, clicks on applicant 
"Maria Santos", inspects the high-resolution scanned Government ID (UMID/Driver's License) 
alongside the live selfie, cross-checks date of birth (confirming legal age of 18+), and 
clicks "Approve Verification".

Output:
The system:
1. Updates the request record under `/verificationRequests/{uid}` to status "Approved" 
   with approval timestamp and administrator UID.
2. Updates `/users/{uid}` in the Realtime Database, setting `isVerified: true`.
3. Dispatches an instant automated system message to Maria's mobile device:
   "Congratulations! Your Save-A-Paws account has been officially verified."
4. Unlocks full access on Maria's mobile profile to adopt pets, schedule retrieval 
   dates, and participate in verified rescue workflows.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 4.2: Stray Emergency Rescue Operations & Photographic Proof Audit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Shelter Administrator / Rescue Coordinator
Purpose:   Auditing field rescue dispatches, verifying proof photos, and tracking 
           stray incidents across the municipality.

Input:
The administrator opens the "Reported Pets in Need" management console on the PC, 
filters reports by status "Rescued", selects Incident #REP-2026-0814, reviews the 
initial distress report photo and GPS coordinates (Marikina Sports Center), inspects 
the field volunteer's submitted live camera rescue proof photo and clinic notes, and 
clicks "Verify & Archive Incident".

Output:
The system:
1. Records official administrative verification on the rescue incident record.
2. Updates the live incident status to "Resolved & Archived", locking the report 
   against further edits.
3. Automatically increments the shelter's public "Rescued Animals" performance metric.
4. Syncs the green resolved status across all connected mobile clients and the 
   community Activity Feed.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 4.3: Shelter Pet Inventory & 5-Character Adoption Pass Verification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Shelter Administrator / Adoptions Manager
Purpose:   Managing adoptable shelter pets and conducting secure on-site handovers.

Input:
Part A (Adding Pet Listing):
Admin clicks "Pet Listings" -> "Add New Pet", uploads photos of rescued cat "Noki", 
enters breed ("Siamese Mix"), age ("1 Year"), gender ("Female"), temperament, and 
toggles medical badges: [x] Vaccinated, [x] Dewormed, [x] Neutered, [x] Microchipped. 
Clicks "Publish Pet".

Part B (On-Site Handover Verification in Marikina):
On retrieval day, the adopter presents their 5-character Adoption Pass code "K9B7X". 
The administrator enters "K9B7X" into the Adoption Pass verification lookup in the 
Admin PC portal, verifies that the retrieval date falls within the mandatory 5-day 
window, validates matching adopter identity, and clicks "Confirm Handover & Complete".

Output:
The system:
1. Validates code "K9B7X", updates pet status to `isAdopted: true`, and links 
   `adoptedBy: adopterUid`.
2. Updates inquiry status to "Completed", logs the physical handover timestamp, and 
   sets `petRetrieved: true`.
3. Automatically generates an in-app signed Adoption Certificate for the adopter and 
   removes the pet from public active search in the Adoption Center.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 4.4: User Account Management, Role Assignment & Disciplinary Moderation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Shelter Administrator
Purpose:   Promoting accredited rescue partners/fosters and moderating platform policy violations.

Input:
Part A (Role Promotion):
Admin navigates to "User Management", searches username "art_metroeast", clicks 
"Edit Role", updates account role from "Volunteer" to "Partner", and clicks "Save Changes".

Part B (Account Moderation / Ban):
Admin identifies an account repeatedly posting commercial pet sales (violating zero-fee 
adoption rules), clicks "Moderate User", selects "Suspend / Ban Account", types reason: 
"Violation of Section 1: Commercial rehoming fee solicitation prohibited", and clicks 
"Confirm Ban".

Output:
The system:
1. Updates the user's role to "Partner" in `/users/{uid}`, unlocking partner rescue 
   badges and listing privileges.
2. For the banned user, sets `isBanned: true` and writes `banReason` to the database.
3. Immediately revokes the banned user's active session; the mobile app presents an 
   account suspension dialog and restricts login.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sample 4.5: Publishing Official Shelter Announcements & Push Notifications
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
User Role: Shelter Administrator / Public Information Officer
Purpose:   Disseminating urgent rabies vaccination drives, pet care workshops, and 
           adoption fair schedules to the public.

Input:
Admin opens "Community & Announcements" on the Admin PC, clicks "Create Announcement", 
enters Title: "Free Community Anti-Rabies Vaccination Drive", Category: "Outreach & Clinic", 
Description: "Save-A-Paws in partnership with City Veterinary Services will conduct free 
anti-rabies vaccination and pet microchipping this Saturday, 9:00 AM at Marikina River Park.", 
uploads an event banner, and clicks "Publish & Broadcast".

Output:
The system:
1. Creates a new timestamped announcement node at `/announcements/{announcementId}`.
2. Instantly publishes the announcement to the Community feed visible to both Guest Users 
   and authenticated members.
3. Triggers real-time in-app notification banners and increments the unread notification 
   badge across all connected mobile application clients.
